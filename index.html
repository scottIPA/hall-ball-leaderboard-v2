HALL BALL CHAMPIONSHIP

Monthly Challenge 2025

How to Play

Roll a ball down the hall once per week to see how close you can get to the end without hitting the door. Each player gets one attempt per week. Players who hit the end wall are disqualified for that week's competition.

Weekly Placements: Numbers show placement each week (1st, 2nd, 3rd, etc.) | "DQ" means disqualified for hitting the wall | "N/A" means no attempt was made

Points System: 1st place: 25pts, 2nd: 18pts, 3rd: 15pts, 4th: 12pts, 5th: 10pts, 6th: 8pts, 7th: 6pts, 8th: 4pts, 9th: 2pts, 10th+: 1pt, Disqualified: 0pts, No attempt: 0pts
